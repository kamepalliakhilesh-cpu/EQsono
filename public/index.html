Pro Tip · Audio Fidelity

Which connection provides the best audio output?

Standard home Wi-Fi suffers from radio interference and buffering delays. For crystal-clear, unbuffered studio sound, use one of these two direct connections:

Rank 1 · Best Sound (0.0ms Jitter)
Direct USB Cable (Single or Multi-Phone)

Physical wire eliminates 100% of Wi-Fi drops and buffering. Sub-millisecond latency (<0.8ms) with lossless CD-quality audio.

How to connect:
  1. Plug your phone(s) into your computer with a USB data cable. (Use a powered USB hub for multiple phones).
  2. On your phone, enable Settings → Hotspot & Tethering → USB Tethering: ON.
  3. In the EQsono Network box, click the USB Tethering IP chip and scan the QR code on your phone.
Rank 2 · Best Wireless (2–5ms Latency)
Laptop 5GHz Mobile Hotspot

Direct PC-to-phone 5GHz wireless without going through a congested home router.

How to connect:
  1. Turn on Mobile Hotspot in Windows Settings (set network band to 5 GHz).
  2. Connect all phones directly to your laptop's hotspot network.
  3. Open http://192.168.137.1:3000/receiver on the connected phones.
