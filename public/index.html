Connection guide

Follow these steps to turn phones into synchronized wireless speakers:

  1. Connect the computer and all mobile devices to the same Wi-Fi router (up to 6 phones).
  2. Scan the QR code or open the receiver URL on each phone.
  3. Tap the speaker activation circle on the phone to enable audio playback.
  4. On this host computer, click Start broadcast and select the audio to stream.
  5. Assign spatial roles in Speakers to calibrate surround sound.
